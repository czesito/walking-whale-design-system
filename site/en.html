No. 02Principles

Three principles that run through it

  • 01

    Precise first. Warm second.

    Hairlines, mono metadata and numbering carry the precision; Sediment and Bone carry the warmth. Cards cast no shadows.

    DR-013

  • 02

    The signature element only appears when it informs

    Growth layers mark steps, pages, reading progress and timelines. Never decoration.

    DR-014

  • 03

    Rules that can be checked get followed

    Contrast, Chinese typography, word choice and component contracts are checked on every push.

    DR-016 · DR-017

No. 03Signature element

Growth layers

A whale’s earplug grows one layer a year; count the layers and you know its age. In the system, each layer is one unit: a step, a page, a stretch of progress.
